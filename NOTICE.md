## Integrity

The installer verifies the downloaded Chrome asset with this SHA-256 checksum:

```text
016817dc815a6c529480ef5d7a0787311a61ec8dc2193f431d68544ee65636fb
```

If the checksum changes, do not load the extension until you have confirmed that the upstream release asset was intentionally updated.
